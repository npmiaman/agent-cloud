# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** open a [discussion](../../discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose) with a reproduction.
- **Security problems:** never in public. Use [private vulnerability reporting](../../security/advisories/new); see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Before asking, check the [docs](docs/) and `GET /llms.txt` on your server. Run
`agentcloud status` and include its output in bug reports.
